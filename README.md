LifeHub — All-in-One Personal Operating System & Daily Dashboard
LifeHub is a clean, modern, and privacy-focused daily management web application designed to help individuals organize, track, and elevate every aspect of their everyday life in one unified interface.
From productivity and task prioritization to health monitoring, habit tracking, personal finance, and real-time weather integration, LifeHub simplifies your daily routine and keeps you focused on what matters most.

🌟 Key Highlights & Features

⚡ Intelligent Home Dashboard

Dynamic Time & Context Greeting: Live real-time clock and adaptive morning/afternoon/evening greetings tailored to your profile.
Daily Motivation: Curated rotating quotes that keep you inspired and disciplined.
At-a-Glance Overview: Unified summary cards for tasks, hydration, steps, budget, and active habits.

🌦️ Automatic Live Climate & Weather Engine

Instant City Search & Auto-Sync: Simply select or type your city, and LifeHub automatically fetches live temperature, weather conditions (Sunny, Cloudy, Rainy, Snow, etc.), humidity, and wind speed.
Unit Customization: Seamless 1-tap toggle between Celsius (°C) and Fahrenheit (°F).
GPS Detection: 1-click current location weather detection.

✅ Smart Task & Priority Management

Organize daily to-dos with priority tags (High, Medium, Low) and categorical labels (Work, Personal, General).
Filter by status, search instantly, and check off achievements with satisfying real-time progress bars.

💧 Health & Wellness Trackers

Hydration Tracker: 1-click water intake logger (+250ml / +500ml) with an animated visual progress ring toward your daily target.
Step & Activity Counter: Real-time tracker for daily steps, distance (km), and estimated calories burned.
Daily Medication Reminders: Schedule daily tablets and check off doses to stay on top of your health regimen.

🔥 Streak & Habit Builder

Track recurring weekly routines and build momentum with clean interactive streak matrices.
Set custom targets per week to build lasting consistency.

💰 Personal Expense & Budget Tracker

Set monthly budgets, log expenses and income with custom currency support ($, ₹, €, £, ¥, AED, etc.).
Visual spending rings, remaining balance calculator, and categorized transaction logs.

📅 Agenda & Quick Scratchpad

Never miss important events, holidays, or deadlines.
Fast instant scratchpad for transient thoughts, draft notes, or quick copy-pasting.

🎨 User Personalization & Design Excellence

Device Gallery Photo Upload: Upload and customize your profile photo directly from your phone/computer gallery.
Dark & Light Mode: Calibrated high-contrast dark and light themes designed for visual comfort and focus.
Cloud Sync & Firebase Backup: Seamless sign-in to back up your data securely to the cloud across devices.
